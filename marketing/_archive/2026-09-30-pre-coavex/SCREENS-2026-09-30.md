## Claims supported by these screens (factual)

- Coach "Today" lists clients by urgency (Urgent / Attention / À surveiller / Bonnes nouvelles). Each row states data observations and one next action, with a one-click "Fait".
- Each signal shows the observation, why it matters, the underlying data and a suggested action; the coach marks it Résolu or Ignorer.
- Each client has prioritised actions (score 0–100) with a stated reason: Fait / Reporter / Ignorer.
- The message assistant prepares an editable draft from the client's data by situation, tone and language. Nothing is sent automatically: the coach sends in the app or copies for WhatsApp. Assisted messages are marked in the thread. Today these drafts come from a rule-based composer, labelled as such.
- Acting (sending, marking done) updates the queue and counters.
- Weekly review per client covers what changed, what went well, what blocked and what to handle, with Approve / Edit / Plan a follow-up / Ignore and a decisions counter.
- Ctrl+K palette with recent clients, client/program search and keyboard shortcuts.
- Per-set logging of reps and load with steppers, previous-session values and a rest timer (show via the crops only until the i18n fix).
- The client sees today's session with targeted muscles, a weight trend and personal records with estimated 1RM.
- It works on a phone (coach Today and queue).

## Claims to AVOID

- "AI manages / coaches / follows up your clients", "AI writes your messages", "powered by Claude/Opus": the AI is **not configured**; drafts are rule-based.
- Any retention, churn or revenue promise ("-X % churn", "never lose a client", "predicts drop-outs"). The UI itself says signals are "une observation, pas une prédiction".
- Any real AI output or result, and any "smart"/"intelligent" framing of the rule composer.
- Real transformations or progress photos: before/after images are SVG silhouettes labelled "Illustration démo".
- Real customers, studios, testimonials or usage numbers: all data is the fictional seed ("Studio Momentum Alger", clients, figures).
- Automatic sending or WhatsApp integration: WhatsApp is **copy only**; nothing is sent automatically.
- "Try the live demo" pointing to coach-os-wine.vercel.app: it serves the old teal skin and old name until redeployed.
- Arabic/RTL screens: the product has an AR locale, but none are captured here.
- Anything medical or diagnostic.

## Product/UX gaps (found while capturing; nothing was changed)

1. **53 i18n keys are used but missing from `src/lib/i18n/dictionary.ts`** (`t()` falls back to the raw key). These are most likely the Forge agents' `i18n` returns that were never merged. They are visible in coach and client workout mode (`workout.*` ×20), client Today (`client.today.weekProgress`, `.stat.sets`, `.stat.time`, `.program`, `.muscles`, `unit.hour`), the client header (`client.coachLabel`), client progress (`client.progress.last8w`), the phone Today (`cc.kpis`), the profile (`common.days28`, `client.coach`), agenda (`agenda.prevWeek/nextWeek`), settings (`settings.*`), error pages (`error.*`), check-in / journey / training / new-client pages. **This blocks the workout, client-Today and client-progress screens.**
2. **The AI badge is misleading when a key is set.** `aiStatus()` reports "IA activée" whenever `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` exists, even when calls fail (no `ANTHROPIC_WORKSPACE_ID`) and drafts fall back to rules. A server with the key, like the live demo presumably, would show "IA activée" beside rule-based drafts. That's why these captures ran with the key blanked.
3. **The rule composer contradicts the thread.** The "Reprendre contact" draft says "Ça fait 12 jours qu'on n'a pas échangé" while Mehdi's message from 3 days earlier sits just above it. It uses app-inactivity days as "échangé".
4. **Plural bug**: "1 actions" in the DONE strip.
5. **"Prochaine séance" can show a past session** ("il y a 5 heures"), and the Today hero keeps a session from 18:00 as "next" late in the evening.
6. **Phone profile is cramped.** Action text wraps into a ~180 px column and the FAB overlaps the contextual action bar.
7. **Seed consistency (Sara)**: her queue row says "Squat 95 kg × 3" while her Records show Squat barre 52.5 kg × 8. Her goal row reads "Squat 100 kg · 62 → 62 kg" (body weight under a strength goal). Don't pair these screens.
8. Screens marketing would want that **do not exist** (not created):
   - an outcome view showing what happened after a coach action (did the client reply or return?);
   - real progress photos (only illustrations);
   - direct WhatsApp sending;
   - a one-glance weekly-review summary (today it is 13 long cards);
   - any real AI copilot output (AI not configured).
9. **Live demo is not on Forge and has the old name**: redeploy is needed before any campaign.

## Re-run (after the COAVEX build is confirmed)

The exact commands are in the header of `marketing/capture-forge.mjs`. In short:
- Seed a fresh SQLite file outside `prisma/` (`prisma db push --skip-generate` + `tsx prisma/seed.ts` with `DATABASE_URL` pointing to it).
- Serve the existing build: `DATABASE_URL=<that file> ANTHROPIC_API_KEY= node node_modules/next/dist/bin/next start -p 3210`.
- Run `node marketing/capture-forge.mjs http://localhost:3210`, then stop the server.

Requirements:
- The script **expects port 3210 and a fresh seeded DB**, never builds, and aborts with exit 2 plus evidence if `--accent` ≠ `EXPECT_ACCENT` (default `#c4f538`; override it if COAVEX changes the lime).
- Output defaults to `marketing/shots-<today>`; set `OUT=` to choose.
- Its selectors rely on the French UI copy listed in the header.

After the run, open every PNG again, re-check `manifest.json` → `rawKeys`, and update this table.
